✦ The Science

Your brain runs on food,
not willpower.

Every emotion you feel is driven by brain chemicals — and every one of those chemicals is built from the food you eat. Here are the four big ones:

😊
Serotonin — the happiness chemical

When it's low you feel sad, flat, or anxious. The surprising bit? 90% of it is made in your gut, not your brain — from something called tryptophan that comes directly from food.

🦃 Turkey🥚 Eggs🌾 Oats🌱 Seeds🍫 Dark Chocolate
🏆
Dopamine — the motivation chemical

Low dopamine = procrastination, zero drive, and reaching for junk food for a quick hit. It's built from an amino acid called tyrosine — found in specific foods. You can genuinely eat your way to more motivation.

🥑 Avocado🍌 Banana🥜 Almonds🫐 Blueberries🍵 Green Tea
😤
Cortisol — the stress hormone

Stress floods your body with cortisol, which drains your magnesium reserves and drives cravings for sugar and fat. Specific foods act as natural brakes — calming the whole system down within hours.

🐟 Salmon🫐 Blueberries🥬 Greens🌿 Ashwagandha🍵 Chamomile
😌
GABA — the calm chemical

GABA is your brain's off switch for anxiety. When it's low you can't switch off — racing thoughts, tension, restlessness. Magnesium and fermented foods directly boost GABA activity.

🥛 Kefir🥬 Spinach🍠 Sweet Potato🥜 Walnuts🫙 Fermented Foods
